# Platform Accountability

> **The bigger the platform, the more it owes the people on it.**

A handful of platforms now sit between billions of people and the information, conversation, and commerce of everyday life. That much power over public life comes with obligations — to be transparent about the rules, honest about how decisions are made, and answerable when the systems cause harm. Too often the obligations are treated as optional. The purpose here is to insist that they aren't.

The work is about accountability as a standard rather than a slogan: shining light on how the largest platforms actually operate, documenting the gap between what they promise and what they do, and pressing for the transparency and responsibility that scale should require. Not anti-technology, and not anti-platform — pro-accountability, on the principle that systems this consequential should answer to the people who live inside them.

Power at this scale should be matched by responsibility at this scale.
